    Welcome to GDAC Lab

    This site introduces the Geometric Dynamics, Autonomy, and Control Laboratory (GDAC Lab) at Nagoya Institute of Technology. We hope it is useful for prospective students, colleagues in academia and industry, and anyone interested in control theory, robotics, and safety-critical systems.

    We develop theory and hardware experiments for constrained control of rotational motion, geometric attitude control on SO(3), and applications such as wheeled drones for infrastructure inspection. A concise overview is on the Research page.

    Prospective students

    How to join the lab as an undergraduate or graduate student will be described on the lab operating guidelines page, which is currently an outline. Guidance on visiting and contacting the lab is also in preparation. To see what we work on, start with Research and People. Formal dates and procedures follow the university's announcements.

    If you are enrolled at a university or graduate school outside Japan, see the exchange programs on NITech's Student Exchange Program page. Applications are often due around five months before the intended start, so please contact the lab as early as possible if you hope to be hosted here through one of them.

    Photos and media: photographs of our equipment and experimental environments are on the Facilities page. Lab activities are posted on our external album (in Japanese).

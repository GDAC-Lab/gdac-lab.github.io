Satellite mesh assets
=====================

  satellite.obj
  satellite.mtl
  satellite.stl

These files were generated from scratch by
examples/satellite/tools/make_satellite_mesh.py, which builds the geometry
out of boxes, cylinders, frustums and a paraboloid.  No third-party model,
scan, texture or CAD data was used, so the files carry no upstream licence.

They are released into the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal:

    https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

To the extent possible under law, the authors have waived all copyright and
related or neighboring rights to these mesh files.  You may copy, modify and
redistribute them, for any purpose, without asking permission and without
attribution.

Everything else in the 3-D scene is likewise generated at run time and carries
no third-party rights: the star field is random, the Earth texture is value
noise evaluated in the browser, and the Sun is a radial gradient drawn on a
canvas.  No external image, font file or model is downloaded by the viewer.
